# 2026-10-29

## Enhancements

### Labels

* The ingredient text on banner unit-dose labels now automatically uses black or white to improve contrast against the configured banner color. Any text color explicitly set in the ingredient formatting is preserved.
* Unit-dose labels now include the pharmacy's phone number when it is configured, alongside the pharmacy address in the compound details.
* New Scoop sizes added for ingredient inventory configuration. The following scoop sizes have been added: 1.25 mL, 9 mL, 14.8 mL, 20 mL, 25 mL, 39 mL.&#x20;

### Data Warehouse

* For pharmacies with data warehouse access, additional Electric Lab data is available for reporting, including ingredient lists, formula lists, formula inventory information, equipment, and lots associated with equipment. These additions help teams connect laboratory information with their reporting workflows.

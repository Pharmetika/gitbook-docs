# 2025-07-30

## Enhancements

* The new **Lots Out of Inventory** report shows formula lots, ingredient lots, or all lots with an inventory of zero or less. It can help you more efficiently manage your inventory and identify products that need to be queued for compounding. From the report, you can click the blue button in the **Lot ID** column to open a specific lot or click the button in the **Remove** column to remove the lot from the report entirely. To remove multiple lots from the report at the same time, select the checkbox for each lot in the **Bulk Remove** column and then click the **Bulk Remove** button.

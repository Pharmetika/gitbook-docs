# 2026-08-07

## **Enhancements**

**Quarantined Queue**&#x20;

* Sterile formula lots that are in quarantine now have a button to flag them as "Out of Specification," where users can enter a case number or note for reference. This flag is visible both on the lot's worksheet and on the quarantine list itself, giving QA better visibility into which batches are under investigation or have an open NC, and supporting daily reporting on what's available for release.
* Quarantined Lots in Electric Lab can now be assigned an “anticipated release date”, editable during or after the "Approve and Quarantine" process. This date now displays alongside lot and quantity information in ION's Dispense Queue, giving non-lab staff visibility into when a quarantined lot is expected to become available for assigning ready-by dates.

**Unit Dose Labels**

* The unit dose label Banner template now automatically picks a contrasting text color based on the background you set. If you want a white or light lime green background, it will now automatically use black text instead of showing white text on a white or light background.

**Reports**

* Formula List Report: Now includes the “Per Unit Formula Pricing” value as a new field called “per unit formula pricing” with that value.

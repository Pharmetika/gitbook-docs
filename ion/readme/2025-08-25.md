# 2025-08-25

## Enhancements

* We’ve revamped the **Request Signature** window to let pharmacy users gather more information about the person picking up and signing for a prescription. Some of this information is required for PMP submissions to certain states; contact your compliance department for more details on what information is required by each state. Now, you can select the relationship of the recipient to the patient, enter their first and last names in separate fields, and note their date of birth and home address. To save the information in this window without requesting the signature, you can click the **Save Handoff Information** button.&#x20;

To see the saved information, open the fill history for the prescription and click **View Handoff Information**.

# 2025-08-13

## Enhancements

* You can now refund individual items from a payment, including an invoice. When you click the **Refund** button in **Patient Payments**, a window appears where you can select which items to refund, how much to refund, and whether the item should be returned to the **Unbilled Items** section after the refund is initiated.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Refund individual items.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* When you create a new prescriber, you can search for existing providers in the SureScripts database and copy over their existing information to the new record, making it a snap to create new prescriber records in ION.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/SS prescriber search.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* You can now see and search by order ID in the **Rx Entry queue**, which can make it easier to find and open the specific prescription you’re looking for. To show the order ID column in the queue, select the **Display Order Column** checkbox in the **Orders** section of the filters. To search by an order ID, enter the ID in the **Order ID** field.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Order ID filter in Rx queue.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* In the **Product Volume By Dispense** report, you can now choose whether each row in the report shows the product that was originally verified in **Prescription Entry** or the product that was ultimately dispensed. This makes it easier to understand what volumes the report is showing and what changes in volume mean for your pharmacy.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Report Date Selection window.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

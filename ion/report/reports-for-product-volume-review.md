# Reports for Product Volume Review

There are three different reports you can use to review your pharmacy's product volume. In these reports, "volume" refers to the amount of product dispensed over a certain period, whether that amount is measured in grams, milliliters, each, or any other unit.

## **Product Volume**

This report shows both compounded and commercial products that were filled 30 days prior to when the report is run, sorted by highest volume first. It is especially useful for forecasting future needs, understanding production trends and cost adjustment needs, and identifying trends in product sales.

This report contains the following columns:

| Column name          | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Product ID           | The system ID of the specific product.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Description          | The name of the product in the system.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Change               | The percent change between the value in the **0-30 Day Volume** column and the **31-60 Day Volume** column. Increased percentages appear in <mark style="color:green;">green</mark> text, decreased percentages appear in <mark style="color:red;">red</mark> text, and 0% changes appear in black text. |
| 0-30 Day Volume      | The total amount of the product filled over the past 30 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 31-60 Day Volume     | The total amount of the product filled over the past 31-60 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 61-90 Day Volume     | The total amount of the product filled over the past 61-90 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 0-30 Day Fill Count  | The total number of times the product was filled over the past 30 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 31-60 Day Fill Count | The total number of times the product was filled over the past 31-60 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 61-90 Day Fill Count | The total number of times the product was filled over the past 61-90 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

## **Product Volume by Date**

This report shows both compounded and commercial products that were filled between the two dates you specify. As with the **Product Volume** report, this report is sorted by highest volume first. It is especially useful for looking at the volume and total number of fills for a certain product in a specific date range.

This report contains the following columns:

| Column name    | Description                                       |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Product ID     | The system ID of the specific product.            |
| Description    | The name of the product in the system.            |
| Date of Record | The date the specific product fill was completed. |
| Quantity       | The quantity required for the fill.               |
| Fill Count     | The fill count number for the fill.               |

## **Product Volume by Dispense**

This report shows both compounded and commercial products that were dispensed 30 days prior to when the report is run, sorted by highest fill count first. It's similar to the **Product Volume** report but specifically excludes the volume of products that were filled but never dispensed to the patient.

This report contains the following columns:

| Column name          | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Product ID           | The system ID of the specific product.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Description          | The name of the product in the system.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Change               | <p>The percent change between the value in the <strong>0-30 Day Volume</strong> column and the <strong>31-60 Day Volume</strong> column. Increased percentages appear in <mark style="color:green;">green</mark> text, decreased percentages appear in <mark style="color:red;">red</mark> text, and 0% changes appear in black text.<br><br>Note that the value in this column might be different from the value in the identically-named column in the <strong>Product Volume</strong> report. This difference is because the <strong>Product Volume</strong> report shows the volume of all fills, while this report shows the volume of products actually dispensed.</p> |
| 0-30 Day Volume      | The total amount of the product dispensed over the past 30 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 31-60 Day Volume     | The total amount of the product dispensed over the past 31-60 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 61-90 Day Volume     | The total amount of the product dispensed over the past 61-90 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 0-30 Day Fill Count  | The total number of times the product was filled over the past 30 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 31-60 Day Fill Count | The total number of times the product was filled over the past 31-60 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 61-90 Day Fill Count | The total number of times the product was filled over the past 61-90 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

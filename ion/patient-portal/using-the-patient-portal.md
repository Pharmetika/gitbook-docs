# Using the Patient Portal

## Send an Invitation to a Patient

1. In ION, open the Patient Profile for the patient you want to invite.
2. In the **Demographics** section, verify that the patient has an email address on file. If they don’t, click the **Edit** button to add one.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Demographics add email (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. In the **Communication Preferences** section, verify that email communication is enabled for the patient, which is true if a small envelope icon appears in the **Email** row. If this icon doesn’t appear, click the icon in the **Set** column to enable it.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Comm preferences enable email (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click the **Send Patient Portal Invite Email** button.

## Registering for the Portal

1. Open the email invitation sent from the pharmacy.
2. Click the **Create Account** button.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Registration email.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Create a password and enter it in both the **Password** and **Confirm Password** fields. This password must contain at least eight characters.
4. Select the **I accept the terms of use** checkbox.
5. Click **Register** to complete the registration process. Your browser automatically redirects you to the login page for the Patient Portal. Make sure to bookmark this login page for easy access.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/registration NEW.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Log In

1. Navigate to the login page.
2. In the **Username** field, enter the username you chose. If you’ve forgotten your username:
   1. Click the **Forgot Username or Password?** link.
   2. Enter your email address in the **Request Username** section.
   3. Click the **Request Username** button to receive an email with your username.
3. In the **Password** field, enter the password you chose. This password must be at least eight characters long. If you’ve forgotten your password:
   1. Click the **Forgot Username or Password?** link.
   2. Enter your username in the **Reset Your Password** section.
   3. Click the **Request Reset** button to receive an email with instructions to reset your password.

## Updating Your Information

After you log in, navigate to the **Update Profile** tab.

* To add a new address in the **Addresses** section, click **Add** (1).
* To edit an existing address in the **Addresses** section, click the arrow to expand the address entry and click **Edit** (2).
* To add an allergy, click **Add** in the **Allergies & Intolerances** section (3).
* To report a new medication, click **Add** in the **Active Medications** section (4).

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Update Info.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Requesting a Medication Refill

1. After you log in, navigate to the **Request Medication** tab (1).
2. Select the checkbox next to the medication you want to refill (2). You can also select the top checkbox to select all listed medications.
3. Click **Request Medication** (3).

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Request Refill.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. In the **Medication Request Confirmation** window, review the medications you’ve selected to refill. If the medications shown are correct, click **Continue**. If you need to add or remove medications, click **Go Back to Prescription Selection**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Medication Request Confirmation.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Select the delivery method you want to use and click **Continue**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Choose a handoff method.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Select the address to which you want the medications to be delivered. If the address you want to use doesn’t appear, you can add it by clicking **Add** or **Edit Addresses**. When you’ve selected the correct address, click **Continue.**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Choose shipping address.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Select the date by which you want to receive your medication and then click **Continue**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Select Date Needed.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. In the **Additional Notes** field, you can add any notes for the pharmacy, including personal preferences or background information. This field is optional. Then, click **Continue**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Additional Notes.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9. Finally, review the details of your medication refill request. If everything looks good, click **Submit Medication Request**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Review everything.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

_In this example, shipping prices are shown to the patient because the prescription is billed directly to the patient. For organization-pay prescriptions, the shipping price does not appear._

10. After the patient submits the request, your staff see a **Patient Refill Request** task in the **Task Queue**. In this task, the staff member can select the **Add to Order** checkbox to create a new order and add this requested medication to that order.

## Review Your Prescription History

After you log in, navigate to the **Prescription History** tab.

* To search for a specific prescription, enter the medication name in the search box (1).
* To review the name of the prescriber and the medication instructions, click the arrow next to the prescription number (2).
* To see the date the prescription was originally written, find the **Date Prescribed** column (3).
* To see the date the prescription was last filled, find the **Last Filled** column (4).

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Prescription History.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Track a Shipment

After you log in, navigate to the **Shipments** tab. Click the shipment you want to review to open the **Shipment Details** window, where you can see the address to which the shipment was sent, the contents of the shipment, the tracking number and carrier, and more.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Shipments tab.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Review Receipts

After you log in, navigate to the **Receipts** tab.&#x20;

* The **Date Processed** column shows the date the payment was processed (1).
* The **Payment Source** column shows the payment method used (2).
* The **Amount** column shows the total amount charged for the transaction (3).

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Payments tab details.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click the payment row to open a detailed receipt for the transaction.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Receipt example.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

# Transfers

You can transfer medications to and from another Pharmetika system electronically, or manually mark prescriptions as transferred to or from external pharmacies after your pharmacist completes a direct phone transfer.

## Controlled Substances

{% hint style="info" %}
It is each individual pharmacist's responsibility to know and understand DEA regulations around transferring prescriptions, including controlled substances.
{% endhint %}

Because controlled substances have restrictions on when, where, and how often they can be transferred, transfer functionality isn't available in scenarios where a controlled substance isn't available to be transferred. For example, under current DEA regulations, controlled substance prescriptions that haven't yet been filled can be transferred on a one-time basis for initial filling. If a prescriber wants to transfer a C3 controlled substance a second time to an external after it has already been transferred, an error message appears when the prescriber attempts to transfer the prescription a second time.

# Transferring Prescriptions to Another Pharmacy

Pharmacies can transfer a prescription out of that pharmacy in either of the following ways:

* At the same time a pharmacist verifies the prescription from **Prescription Entry.**
* From the prescription's entry in the **Prescription History** section of the **Patient Summary**.&#x20;

To transfer a prescription at verification from **Prescription Entry:**

1. Open the prescription in **Prescription Entry.**
2. Below the green **Mark Verified** button on the right side of the screen, select the **Transfer Upon Verification checkbox.** Not that when this option is selected, the **Profiled Only** and **Fill Upon Verification** checkboxes are automatically disabled.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Transfer from Rx Entry.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. If you're transferring the prescription to another pharmacy using Pharmetika with whom you are associated or in partnership, select that pharmacy's realm in the **Transferring Realm** field. If you're transferring the prescription to an external pharmacy, select External Pharmacy from the **Transferring Realm** field.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Transferring realm.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
To add a realm to the transferring list, request support at education@pharmetika.com.
{% endhint %}

3. For external pharmacy transfers, click the **Enter External Pharmacy Details button** and fill out the form that appears, then click **Save.** Transferring electronically to another partner pharmacy's realm doesn't require the user to enter additional pharmacy information.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (564) (1).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Verify the prescription as usual by clicking the **Mark Verified button**. When this action is complete, a prescription number is generated and the prescription is marked as discontinued on the **Patient Summary** page.
